# Clankvasion

## Project Description
**Clankvasion** is a hybrid tower defense shooter set in a cyberpunk world where robots have turned against humanity. 

Players defend a central Main Tower by manually aiming and shooting enemies while buying and placing defensive towers. Enemies come from all directions. Survive and upgrade your Main Tower to Level 5 to win.

## How to Play

- **Mouse** = Aim the Main Tower's gun
- **Left Click** = Shoot
- Kill enemies to earn currency
- Buy and place defensive towers
- Upgrade your Main Tower up to Level 5 to unlock the Nuke and win
- If the Main Tower's health reaches 0, you lose

---

## Gameplay Features

- Manual shooting from the Main Tower
- Tower purchasing and free placement
- Main Tower upgrading (up to Level 5)
- Enemies approaching from all directions
- Currency system (earned by killing enemies)
- Multiple tower types with unique abilities
- Pause menu and upgrade menu

---

## Towers

**Main Tower (Player Controlled)**  
Upgradable central tower with increasing damage, fire rate, and extra guns. Level 5 unlocks Nuke (win condition).

**Mine** – Cheap, area damage on contact (single use)  
**Water Tank** – Medium cost, damage over time in an area  
**EMP** – Stuns enemies in an area for 5 seconds (single use)  
**Trash Compactor** – High cost, pulls in and destroys one enemy

---

## Enemies

- **Roller** – Fast, low health
- **Clank** – Slow, tanky
- **Clink** – Fast, medium health
- **Bull** – Medium speed, high health
- **Clunk** – Fast and tanky (high threat)

All enemies move toward the Main Tower and deal 1 damage on contact.

---

## How to Run

1. Open the project in Unity
2. Open `MainGame.unity` scene
3. Press Play

**Main Controls**: Mouse to aim, Left Click to shoot, ESC to pause.
